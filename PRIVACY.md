# Privacy

The skills in this repository are text files. They run inside the AI assistant you use (Claude Code, Claude.ai, Cowork or another assistant), on the files you attach and the connectors you have turned on yourself. This plugin has no server, no MCP server and no code of its own.

## What the skills read

The exports you attach: Campaign Manager exports, web analytics exports and CRM exports. A CRM export can hold personal data, such as contact names, job titles and email addresses. The skills read it only to build their recommendations in your conversation.

## What the skills send or keep

Nothing. No skill sends data anywhere, and neither DemandSense nor the skill authors receive, store or see your files, your conversations or your results. What happens to your conversation is governed by your assistant's own terms, for Claude by [Anthropic's privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy).

## The DemandSense MCP, if you use it

Some skills can read your accounts through the DemandSense MCP instead of exports. That is a separate DemandSense product you connect yourself, covered by the [DemandSense privacy policy](https://demandsense.com/privacy), not by this file.
